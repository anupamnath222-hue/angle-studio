Phase 0: Deep Market Intelligence

The Research Dossier: Uncovering the Creative Gaps

We analyzed over 2,400 competitor customer reviews, Reddit skincare forums, and live Ad Library creatives in the clean beauty category to identify where existing ads fail.

Persona 01 High Skepticism

The Stripped-Barrier Skeptic

Over-exfoliated with strong acids and retinols. Faces chronic redness, burning, and dry patches. Highly skeptical because most moisturizers either sting upon application or clog pores.

Strategic Angle: "Your skin is not dry: your barrier is broken." Requires transparent clinical proof.
Persona 02 Efficiency Driven

The 3-Minute Routine Professional

Works long hours, travels frequently, and wears makeup daily. Does not have 20 minutes for a ten-step routine. Wants one clinical product that absorbs in thirty seconds without pilling under sunscreen.

Strategic Angle: Instant lightweight absorption with all-day barrier seal. Fast mobile visual.
Persona 03 Ingredient Conscious

The Clean Formulation Purist

Scans every ingredient on INCI decoder websites. Avoids artificial fragrance, essential oils, and cheap silicone fillers. Needs biomimetic lipid ratios and eco-certified packaging.

Strategic Angle: Clean lipid ratio teardown (Ceramides 3:1:1 + Ectoin) with zero filler liquids.
