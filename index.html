Got Questions?

Everything you need to know.

Why is creative the only lever that matters under Meta Andromeda?

Meta's retrieval engine has automated ad-set bidding and audience selection. The algorithm reads the visual and audio content of your ad to determine who to show it to. When you launch a new creative angle (e.g. an ingredient teardown vs. a problem agitation hook), you are unlocking an entirely new pool of buyers. Changing audience settings manually can no longer accomplish this.

What do you mean by "unlimited static angle matrices"?

Many agencies charge per static graphic. We don't. We know that static ads are the fastest, most cost-effective way to test 10+ psychological angles in 48 hours. Once we find the hook that drives high link CTR and low CPC, we immediately turn that winning angle into a full 3D motion or UGC video series.

How do you handle both Physical Products and B2B/Services?

We operate two distinct creative pipelines. For physical products (DTC, fashion, FMCG), we deploy 3D CGI unboxings, sensory macro hooks, and mechanism teardowns. For services and B2B, we deploy authority breakdowns, problem agitation reels, whiteboard explainers, and interactive advertorial assets.

Do we get open source files and full ownership?

Yes, 100%. Every deliverable includes complete open source files: Figma project files, After Effects project packages, raw 4K MP4 exports, and high-res PNG static suites. You own everything we create.

How does the 72-hour turnaround work in practice?

You drop a brief into your dedicated Slack channel — whether it's a 2-minute Loom, a link to a competitor's ad, or raw bullet points. Within 24 hours, we return the strategic angle blueprint. Within 48–72 hours, final production-ready assets are delivered and ready to launch in Ads Manager.
