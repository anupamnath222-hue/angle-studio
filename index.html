Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 72-hour turnaround work? ▼

Once you drop your brief into Slack, our team gets to work immediately. Static ad angle sets and social video cuts are delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Complex custom 3D animations are shared in visual stages so you always see progress.

What kind of assets do we need to give you? ▼

You don't need a huge production folder. A link to your website, raw customer reviews, your existing photo folder, or a quick 2-minute Loom walkthrough is plenty. If you have brand guidelines, we follow them to the pixel.

What if we need revisions? ▼

Revisions are unlimited. If an asset isn't exactly what you wanted, simply drop a comment in Figma or Slack. We make the adjustments quickly until you are completely satisfied.

Do we own the source files? ▼

Yes, 100%. You own everything we create: open Figma files, After Effects project packages, 4K MP4 video exports, and static PNG assets.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription? ▼

Yes. You can pause your subscription anytime with zero penalty. If your product is out of stock or you want to pause ad spend, you never pay for unused time.
